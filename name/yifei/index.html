Yīfēi (依斐) — Clouds piled softly against the eaves

Yīfēi · 依斐（依斐）

Layered soft clouds. The Chu poets imagined clouds gathering so gently they touch the rooftops — a name that feels like home and sky at once.

云依斐而承宇，虹霓纷其朝霞。
“Clouds layer softly against the eaves; rainbows tangle in the dawn light.”
— 《楚辞·哀时命》 ('Lamenting the Times', Songs of Chu tradition), Han dynasty

What Yīfēi means

Layered soft clouds. The Chu poets imagined clouds gathering so gently they touch the rooftops — a name that feels like home and sky at once.

The poem behind the name

The line comes from 《楚辞·哀时命》 ('Lamenting the Times', Songs of Chu tradition), Han dynasty. In the original: “云依斐而承宇，虹霓纷其朝霞。”. Clouds layer softly against the eaves; rainbows tangle in the dawn light.

The characters, idea by idea

依 is a person (亻) leaning on 衣 'clothing' — to rely on, to lean close, to be tender. 斐 pairs the literary radical (文) twice over: patterned, elegant, accomplished.

Who this name suits

Yīfēi suits someone gentle, artistic, and dependable — the soft-spoken creative whose work is quietly impeccable. Feminine-leaning, graceful, modern-feeling with ancient roots.

Common Questions

Is Yīfēi a common Chinese name?

依 is a popular modern name element; 依斐 is rare and literary, known mostly to readers of the Chu anthology.

How do you pronounce 依斐?

It is pronounced “ee-FAY” in Mandarin (Yīfēi with tone marks). Non-speakers can say it as written and Chinese friends will understand and smile.

Can a foreigner use 依斐 as a Chinese name?

Yes — and it is a beautiful choice. Unlike translated names, 依斐 is a real poetic name with a story you can tell in one sentence. 依斐 is written identically in simplified and traditional Chinese. 斐 is also the character in 斐然 'strikingly brilliant' — an auspicious echo.
