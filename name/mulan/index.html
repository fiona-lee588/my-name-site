Mùlán (沐兰) — Bathing in orchid water, blessed and pure

Mùlán · 沐兰（沐蘭）

Bathing in orchids. Before the cloud god descends, he bathes in orchid-scented water and dresses in radiance — purity, blessing, and freshness.

浴兰汤兮沐芳，华采衣兮若英。
“Bathing in orchid water, anointing with sweet herbs, robed in splendor like living flowers.”
— 《楚辞·九歌·云中君》 (Songs of Chu, 'The Lord of the Clouds'), c. 300 BCE

What Mùlán means

Bathing in orchids. Before the cloud god descends, he bathes in orchid-scented water and dresses in radiance — purity, blessing, and freshness.

The poem behind the name

The line comes from 《楚辞·九歌·云中君》 (Songs of Chu, 'The Lord of the Clouds'), c. 300 BCE. In the original: “浴兰汤兮沐芳，华采衣兮若英。”. Bathing in orchid water, anointing with sweet herbs, robed in splendor like living flowers.

The characters, idea by idea

沐 is a head (页) under falling water (氵) — to bathe and to be blessed. 兰 (蘭) is the orchid, the noblest of plants in Chinese culture, 'the fragrance of the gentleman.'

Who this name suits

Mùlán suits someone fresh-hearted and honorable, who makes every room feel cleaner by being in it. Lovely for a girl; dignified for a wellness or beauty brand.

Common Questions

Is Mùlán a common Chinese name?

兰 has been a cherished name character for centuries; 沐兰 is uncommon enough to feel newly discovered, and carries no relation to the soldier Mulan legend — this name is older.

How do you pronounce 沐兰?

It is pronounced “moo-LAHN” in Mandarin (Mùlán with tone marks). Non-speakers can say it as written and Chinese friends will understand and smile.

Can a foreigner use 沐兰 as a Chinese name?

Yes — and it is a beautiful choice. Unlike translated names, 沐兰 is a real poetic name with a story you can tell in one sentence. Simplified 沐兰 / traditional 沐蘭. 蘭 keeps the grass radical (艹) around 門 'gate' and 柬 — the orchid standing at the gate of a noble house.
